# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and professional collaboration
environment.

Examples of expected behavior:

- giving useful, technically grounded feedback
- respecting different levels of experience
- assuming good intent while reviewing critically
- focusing discussion on the work, not the person

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- bad-faith disruption of project discussions
- publication of private information without permission

## Maintainer Responsibility

Maintainers may moderate discussions, reject contributions, or remove access
when behavior harms the project or its community.

## Reporting

Security matters should go through `SECURITY.md`.

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers through the
organization contact path or repository security contact once configured.
